01. Professional Profile & About Me
OVERVIEW

I am a passionate and motivated first-year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate student at JECRC University, Jaipur, hailing from Balotra, Rajasthan, India. My journey into mechanical engineering is driven by an inquisitive curiosity about how physical mechanisms, power transmission systems, and industrial machines operate.

As an aspiring engineer early in my academic formation, I am committed to building rigorous core engineering fundamentals in mechanics, engineering design, manufacturing processes, and modern computer-aided engineering. I believe in combining theoretical principles with disciplined practical inquiry, problem-solving, and continuous learning to contribute meaningfully to emerging engineering challenges.
